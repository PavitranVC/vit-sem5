Cloud Architecture Design (AWS) BCSE355L - exam Fri 13 Nov FN

5 FAT papers (Nov 2024 to Nov 2025), 10 questions x 10 marks. Every paper has exactly one question from each module and several have a second. Answers are scenarios: restate requirement, name services, draw connections, justify (the 4-line template). Added 9 Nov: your teacher's CAT-2 DA lists 8 questions, all from Modules 7 and 8 (SQS vs SNS, fan-out, SWF vs Step Functions, Standard vs Express, six pillars, server migration, DMS, Snow family). Treat them as likely FAT questions; page map in _prep/study-guide/CAD-slide-map.md.

Either/or questions: Two either/or questions per paper. M8 has one in 4 of 5 papers (Snow family is always one side). M5, M7 each in 2 of 5; M3, M6 in 1 of 5. M1, M2 and M4 never have a choice.
Do these first (Must-priority, most often asked):
  1. VPC: public and private subnets, IGW, NAT, route tables, SG (M3, 5/5 papers)
  2. IAM users, groups, roles, policies, least privilege (M1, 4/5 papers)
  3. RDS Multi-AZ and read replicas (M4, 4/5 papers)
  4. CloudWatch + CloudTrail + Config together (M5, 4/5 papers)
  5. SQS, SNS, Step Functions, SWF (M7, 4/5 papers)
  6. Snow family (Snowball, Edge, Snowmobile) (M8, 4/5 papers)

Operating Systems BCSE303L - exam Sun 15 Nov FN

7 FAT papers (Jul 2023 to Apr/May 2026). Most are 10 x 10; the May 2024 and Apr 2026 papers use 4 x 10 + 4 x 15. Question order is stable: Q1 structure, Q2 processes/threads, Q3 scheduling, Q4 Banker's, Q5-6 synchronisation, Q7-8 memory, Q9 files/virtualisation, Q10 disk/protection.

Either/or questions: Either/or appears at the end of the paper: Q9 and Q10 in B2'24 and C1'25; paging-vs-address-calculation and ACL-vs-virtualisation in C2'25; Q3 and Q5 in B1'24. So files/virtualisation (Q9) and disk scheduling/protection (Q10) usually give you a choice.
Do these first (Must-priority, most often asked):
  1. OS structure, design issues, services (M1, 7/7 papers)
  2. CPU scheduling Gantt chart, WT, TAT (M3, 7/7 papers)
  3. Banker's algorithm / deadlock detection (M3, 7/7 papers)
  4. Disk scheduling total head movement (M7, 7/7 papers)
  5. Page replacement FIFO / LRU / Optimal (M5, 5/7 papers)
  6. File allocation methods (M6, 5/7 papers)
  7. Virtualisation / hypervisors (M6, 5/7 papers)

Artificial Intelligence BCSE306L - exam Thu 19 Nov FN

6 FAT papers (Nov 2024 x2, May 2024, Nov 2025 x2, one 2025 G2 read partly from a photo). 10 x 10 marks. In the 2025 papers the order is fixed: Q1 agents, Q2-3 search, Q4 minimax, Q5-6 logic, Q7-8 probability, Q9 planning (OR), Q10 NLP/IR/vision (OR).

Either/or questions: Q9 and Q10 are either/or in E1, E2, D1 and D2. In 2025 the Q9 choice is between two planning questions (STRIPS vs HTN) and the Q10 choice is between information retrieval and object recognition / language models, so learn one of each pair properly. B1'24 lets you answer any 10 of 12.
Do these first (Must-priority, most often asked):
  1. Greedy best-first + A* on a graph (M2, 5/6 papers)
  2. FOL sentences + resolution proof (M4, 5/6 papers)
  3. STRIPS representation / plan (M6, 5/6 papers)
  4. PEAS for a scenario (M1, 4/6 papers)
  5. Minimax + alpha-beta pruning (M3, 4/6 papers)
  6. Forward / backward chaining (M4, 4/6 papers)
  7. Bayes theorem numericals (M5, 4/6 papers)
  8. Bayesian network inference (M5, 4/6 papers)
  9. Hierarchical task network (HTN) planning (M6, 4/6 papers)
  10. Information retrieval / ranking (M7, 4/6 papers)
